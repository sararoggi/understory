About the project

Understory is an NLP project exploring the presence and agency of the natural world in literary texts. The model was fine-tuned on Romantic-era English literature to identify non-human natural entities and classify them into five categories:

  • Flora: Trees, flowers, moss, woods, foliage
  • Fauna: Animals, birds - literal, non-human creatures
  • Weather: Weather, atmospheric, and celestial phenomena
  • Landscape: Topographic and geological features
  • Nature: Personified nature-as-force

Then, a dependency-based grammatical analysis examines the role each one plays within its sentence. Rather than simply counting references to nature, it looks at how these entities are positioned within the language of a text: whether they appear as subjects, objects, or in other grammatical roles, revealing the different ways the natural world participates in the events of the text.

By combining named entity recognition with grammatical analysis, Understory offers a way to explore not only which forms of nature appear in a text, but also how they are represented through language.
